Decoding Stars - Amitabh Bachchan • September 2026

Taking a look at Amitabh Bachchan's Astrological Profile-Part 1

By Vinayak Krupa Astrology • 10 min read

The story of Amitabh Bachchan's life is not simply one of success; it is a remarkable journey involving struggle, great fame, setbacks, financial difficulties, health problems, and a strong comeback.
Starting from failure in the film industry and going on to become one of India's most iconic actors, his life serves as an interesting case study from the point of view of Vedic astrology.

The Yogas in the Horoscope of Amitabh Bachchan and how they shape his destiny

The major Yogas in Amitabh Bachchan's Horoscope are:

  • Anapha Yoga
  • Sakala Vipareet Raj Yoga
  • Raj Yoga
  • Gajakesari Yoga
  • Budha-Aditya Yoga
  • Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga

Let us consider what these yogas mean and how their characteristics can be connected with the personality and life journey of Amitabh Bachchan.

1. Anapha Yoga,The ability to create an identity of one's own

Anapha Yoga occurs when a planet apart from the Sun is located in the 12th house from the Moon.
This kind of yoga is usually linked with self-reliance, independence, confidence, mental strength and the capacity to form one's own identity.
The quality is very clearly evident in Amitabh Bachchan's early career.
He didn't become successful all at once; on the contrary, in the early years of his career in the film industry he was rejected and criticised. At first, his height, look and deep voice were not seen as typical characteristics of a hero in Hindi films.
Rather than altering himself to suit the existing pattern, he in the end made use of these same characteristics as his main strengths.

His deep voice became his trademark, his tall stature gave him an advantage, and his serious on-screen presence contributed to the creation of the famous "Angry Young Man" image.

2. Sakala Vipareet Raj Yoga, turning difficulties into success

A principle among the most intriguing in Vedic astrology is Vipareet Raj Yoga.
Vipareet consists of the word "opposite", and the fundamental concept is that some difficult planetary arrangements are able to bring about beneficial outcomes exactly because of the difficulties and obstacles they present.
Rather than leading to a perfectly smooth life, this form of yoga can cause a person to go through tough situations which in the end serve as stepping stones to success.
The case of Amitabh Bachchan is a striking example of this pattern.

His journey included:
Struggle during the early years of his career
Extraordinary success in the 1970s
Serious health challenges
Financial and professional difficulties
A period of uncertainty
A remarkable comeback through television and films

What is important is that he never fell.
Each fall was then followed by a rise. One of the best methods of grasping the practical significance of Vipareet Raj Yoga is this.
It is precisely the circumstances which cause difficulties that end up being the origin of a person's greatest strength.

3. Raj Yoga, the ability to rise to the top

The fact that Raj Yoga doesn't necessarily mean becoming a king in a literal sense. Raj Yoga in astrology is connected with status, authority, recognition, influence, success and holding a respected position in society.
Amitabh Bachchan's life is a remarkable example of such qualities.

He moved from being a struggling actor to becoming:
One of the most respected actors in Indian cinema
A national cultural icon
A highly influential public personality
A successful television host
A recipient of major national and international honours
His fame reached far beyond the realm of cinema as well.

The fact that he was associated with Kaun Banega Crorepati (KBC) brought him into contact with a completely new generation and once more changed the course of his public image.
This shows one of the key features of Raj Yoga—that recognition can vary from stage to stage in one's life.

4. Gajakesari Yoga, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Influence

The traditional formation of Gajakesari Yoga is due to a close relationship between Jupiter and the Moon, especially when they are in a Kendra aspect.

It is considered an important yoga associated with:

Intelligence
Wisdom
Reputation
Social respect
Popularity
Good judgement
Influence over others

It is especially interesting to note Amitabh Bachchan's ability to communicate with people from different generations.
The reason for his success in hosting KBC was not merely due to his celebrity status. His use of language, sense of humour, sense of timing, ability to listen and the way in which he interacted with the contestants all became an important factor in the show's success. As he got older, his public image also became more dignified and mature.

It is therefore possible to meaningfully examine the qualities usually linked with Gajakesari Yoga—namely wisdom, respect and influence—in connection with his later public life.

Just like with any other form of yoga, its true strength can only be assessed by referring to the entire horoscope, taking into account planetary dignity, aspects, house ownership, conjunctions and Dashas.

5. Budha-Aditya Yoga — intelligence, communication and powerful expression

Budha-Aditya Yoga results from the conjunction of Mercury and the Sun.

Mercury represents:

Intelligence
Speech
Communication
Analytical ability
Learning
Adaptability

The Sun represents:

Confidence
Authority
Leadership
Recognition
Individuality

When the two planets come together, the individual might acquire a strong ability to express ideas, to communicate effectively and to apply their intelligence in practical situations.
It is especially interesting in the case of Amitabh Bachchan since his voice and his skills in communicating became an essential element of his identity. His way of delivering dialogue in films is well known.
His ability to communicate became still more apparent on KBC, since there he had to respond spontaneously to the contestants, understand their emotions, use humour and keep the attention of millions of viewers.
One other key feature of Mercury is its adaptability.

Bachchan successfully adapted to different phases of the entertainment industry: Cinema → Television → Advertising → Digital platforms → Contemporary cinema
One of the most remarkable aspects of his career is his ability to reinvent himself.

6. The yoga of the Dharma-Karmadhipati — when good luck aids one's career

Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga is especially important when it comes to the analysis of a person's career among the yogas listed.
The ninth house stands for dharma, fortune, blessings, higher principles and destiny. The tenth house stands for karma, one's profession, career, social status and public achievements.

A Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga is formed when the lords of the 9th and 10th houses enter into a significant relationship.

For a Kumbha (Aquarius) Ascendant:

9th lord is Venus
10th lord is Mars
In the widely used chart concerning Amitabh Bachchan, Venus and Mars are placed in Virgo.
The basic principle of this yoga is fascinating: When your actions coincide with your fate, diligent work can result in outstanding recognition.
Amitabh's career shows this over and over again.

Success was not merely waiting for him; it was due to his persistent effort, discipline, and his ability to keep working through difficult times
